How to Use PocketDesk Remote

Follow these 3 easy steps to control your PC from anywhere with $0 budget.

1

Scan QR from your Mobile Phone

Open your normal smartphone camera (iPhone or Android), point it at the QR code in the Connect tab, and tap the link.

2

Add to Home Screen (Optional)

In your mobile browser (Safari/Chrome), tap Share / Menu → "Add to Home Screen". It now opens full-screen without address bars, just like a native app!

Use your phone screen as a Trackpad (tap = click, two fingers = right click or scroll), tap the Keyboard icon to type, and access quick keys like Win, Alt+Tab, and Ctrl+C.
